# Yinyang Drop privacy policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy applies to the Android app Yinyang Drop (package `com.tropiclabs.yinyangdrop`), published by Tropic Labs.

## Summary

- Yinyang Drop has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
- The app does not have the Android `INTERNET` permission. It does not send data to Tropic Labs or to other companies.
- The app keeps your scenes and settings on your device.

## The data that the app keeps on your device

The app keeps this data in its private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read this storage.

- **Scenes.** The scenes that you make, change or duplicate: the name of the scene, the container, the theme, the background and the motion setting.
- **Containers.** The containers that you draw for a scene.
- **Thumbnails.** Small pictures of the scenes, for the scene list.
- **Settings.** The scene in use, the flow speed, the frame rate and the tilt setting.

Tropic Labs does not get a copy of this data.

## The motion sensor

When the "Tilt" setting is on, the app reads the accelerometer of your device while the scene or the wallpaper is visible. The liquid then follows the tilt of the device. The app uses the readings only in memory, for the animation. It does not save or send them. When "Tilt" is off, the app does not read the sensor.

## Backups and device transfers

The app turns off the Android cloud backup of its data. When you move to a new device, Android can copy the app data with a device-to-device transfer. Tropic Labs does not get a copy.

## How long the data stays, and how to delete it

The data stays on your device until you delete it.

- To delete a scene, open the scene in "Customize" and tap "Delete".
- To delete all the data of the app, open the Android settings of the app and clear its storage, or uninstall the app.

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from any user, including children.
